**I am on the 2026–2027 job market.**

I am a PhD candidate in Public Policy at the University of Chicago Harris School studying topics in labor and public economics. My dissertation project examines the rise in non-employment among prime-aged men.

I have taught across undergraduate, master's, and doctoral courses in economics, statistics, and programming. I also advise undergraduate senior theses as a preceptor. In 2026, I received the Harris School's Outstanding Teaching Assistant of the Year Award.

Before my PhD, I was a pre-doctoral fellow at the University of Chicago, and I received my A.B. in Economics and Mathematics from Dartmouth College.
